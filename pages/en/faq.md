  - q: Why use sustainable materials in everyday products?
    a: |
      Making everyday products from sustainable materials reduces environmental impact and supports corporate social responsibility policies. It shows a company's commitment to people and the planet, strengthens the brand and earns consumer trust.

      Sustainable materials also support long-term environmental goals by reducing waste and pollution. Choosing low-carbon, recyclable or biodegradable materials lowers a company's carbon footprint and keeps it in step with international carbon regulation.

      Choosing sustainable materials is the best way to meet both your responsibilities and market demand.
