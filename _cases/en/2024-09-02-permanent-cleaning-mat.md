The permanent decontamination floor mat is designed for semiconductor cleanrooms, pharmaceutical and food plants, manufacturing sites, clinics and any environment that demands low contamination.

It effectively reduces area contamination, cuts waste and lowers the risk of pathogen spread. Recyclable and highly load-bearing, it can be made in custom colours and sizes — a durable, sustainable solution.
